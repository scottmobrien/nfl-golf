⛳ The Daily 9 #1

Welcome to NHL Golf - Let’s Tee it Up!

How to play:

  • Select a game mode and the number of holes you’d like to play, or take a shot at the Daily 9.
  • Each “hole” is an NHL statistical category (e.g. career assists), and the “pin” is a spot on that list (e.g. #75 out of 250).
  • The number of guesses you get depends on the par of the hole
    • Par 3: one guess - your Tee Shot.
    • Par 4: two guesses - your Tee Shot and an Approach.
    • Par 5: three guesses - your Tee Shot, a Second Shot and an Approach.
  • Each shot is a guess at a player you think is as close as possible to the pin.
    • Name the exact player (or one of the players tied at that rank) and you’re in the hole. Do it off the tee and it’s a hole in one, on any par!
    • After your Tee Shot (and your Second Shot on a par 5), you’ll see the players ranked near your guess. Use them to line up your next shot.
    • If your last shot misses, you putt out. The closer your last shot is to the pin, the fewer putts you need. Remember that the green is based on the size of the list: on a 250-player list you can be further away from the pin and still make par than you could on a 50-player list.
    • Guess a player who isn’t on the list and you’re out of bounds: +1 penalty stroke, or +5 if it’s your last shot.
  • Stuck? Ask the Caddy to see who’s 30% of the list away from the pin in either direction, and take a +1 stroke penalty.
  • Share your results with friends, and play the Daily 9 for a spot on the leaderboard. Enjoy!
