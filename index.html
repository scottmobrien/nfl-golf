Welcome to NFL Golf - Let’s Tee it Up!

How to play:

  • Select a game mode and the number of holes you’d like to play, or take a shot at the daily 9
  • Each “hole” is an NFL statistical category (e.g. rushing touchdowns), and the “pin” is a random spot on that list (e.g. #75 out of 250)
  • Guess a player who you think is as close as possible to that spot on the list
    • If you guess the exact player (or one of the players tied for that ranking), that’s a hole in one!
    • The closer your chosen player is to that ranking, the better your score on that hole - and remember that your score is a function of the size of the list. On a list of 250 players, the “green” will be larger and you can make par by being further away from the target number than you could on a list that only has 50 players.
  • Share your results with friends and play the daily 9 for a spot on the leaderboard. Enjoy!
