Tee it up.

Each hole is an NFL stat leaderboard. The pin is a random rank on that list. Name a player who sits as close to that rank as you can. Your score on the hole is how far off you were as a percentage of the list. Lowest total wins.

  • Example: Hole is Rushing Yards, pin is #10. The list has 250 players and you guess someone ranked #15 → 5 spots off → 2 points.
  • Name someone who isn't on the list (most go about 250 deep) → out of bounds: 100 points, the max.
  • Tied players share a rank.
